# 14. License egon-mcp under GPL-3.0-or-later

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-17
- Context: The server builds Egon's GPLv3 icons, most of them based on Apache-2.0 Material Icons, into its output and exists to serve Egon users. The owner wants the same terms as Egon.
- Decision: egon-mcp uses Egon's terms, the GNU GPL version 3 or any later version, with the full text in `LICENSE` at the repository root.
- Consequences: Anyone may use, change and share the server, and copies or changed versions they distribute must stay under the GPL with source.
